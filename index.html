GOOD TO KNOW

A few FAQs.

Everything you need to know about this demo event platform.

Is this a real event-booking website? +

No. K Explore is a student web-development project. Events, venues, and registration examples are fictional, and no real seats are booked.

Where is my registration information saved? +

Form submissions are saved only in this browser's localStorage. They are not sent to a server, emailed, or shared with an event organizer. Avoid entering sensitive information on shared devices.

Can I view or delete my registration? +

Yes. Select “My registrations” in the top menu to view saved demo registrations, export a CSV file, or remove any entry.

Does the site work on mobile devices? +

Yes. The layout and navigation are responsive, designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
